Privacy Policy — Purrhaps Sinbi Cat Watch Face

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Rolling Mango Corp (the "Company") publishes the Wear OS watch face "Purrhaps Sinbi Cat Watch Face" (package co.rollingmango.purrhaps.watchface, the "Watch Face"). This policy applies to the Watch Face only. The Purrhaps phone app has its own Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Watch Face does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It contains no code of its own, requests no permissions, has no network access, shows no ads, and has no analytics or user accounts.

2. Data Shown on the Watch Face

The Watch Face shows the time and date from your watch. The three complication slots (battery, steps, and next event by default) display what the data sources you pick on your watch provide, such as the Wear OS system or your calendar app. That data is handled by those data sources on your watch under their own policies; the Watch Face does not read it for any other purpose or send it anywhere.

3. Settings

Your choice of Sinbi's pose and complication data sources is kept on your watch by the Wear OS system. The Company does not receive it.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the Watch Face collects no personal data, it does not collect personal information from children either.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries: info@rollingmango.co.kr

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
